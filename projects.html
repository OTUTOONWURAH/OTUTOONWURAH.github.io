GRC Risk Decision Simulation

Project Overview

In this team-based cybersecurity simulation, I evaluated the risks of keeping an outdated server that was important to a company's inventory and ordering operations. Our team balanced cybersecurity risks with the potential business disruption of replacing the system during a busy sales period.

My Contribution

I helped evaluate the security and business risks of the outdated server and contributed to our team's decision to temporarily mitigate the risk. I recommended restricting network access, monitoring for unusual activity, and maintaining reliable backups while preparing for a future system upgrade.

Risk Treatment

MITIGATE

Our team decided to temporarily mitigate the risk instead of immediately replacing the server. We recommended network segmentation, restricted administrative access, increased monitoring, and reliable backups. After the busy business period, the outdated server should be replaced or upgraded with proper testing and recovery planning.

What I Learned

This simulation helped me understand that cybersecurity decisions must consider both security and business needs. I learned how organizations can temporarily mitigate risk while preparing for a more permanent solution.
